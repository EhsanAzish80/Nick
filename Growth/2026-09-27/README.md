# Nick focused growth cycle

This folder turns Nick 4.6.2 into a measurable three-week growth experiment.
The objective is not maximum posting volume. It is to find which audience and
proof produces qualified downloads, useful feedback, and contributors without
weakening trust in a security product.

## Sequence

### Week 1 — clarity and instrumentation

- Ship the README, onboarding, and product-page positioning changes from the audit.
- Capture GitHub and release-download baselines in `tracking.csv`.
- Record a 45–60 second real-app demo and one Deep Scan progress clip.
- Label or draft five bounded contributor issues.
- Add campaign parameters to every channel-specific website link.

### Week 2 — two controlled distribution tests

- Publish one technical architecture article on 3NSofts and share it from the
  founder account.
- Make one feedback-led community post in the single best eligible channel.
- Answer replies and issues; do not cross-post the same pitch.
- Take the first weekly measurement seven full days after the baseline.

### Week 3 — conversion and contribution

- Publish the measured false-positive case study.
- Test one second audience/channel only if week-two feedback supports it.
- Improve the product page or onboarding at the largest observed drop-off.
- Close with a short findings report: keep, change, stop, and next experiment.

## Guardrails

- No unsolicited direct messages, paid engagement, manufactured reviews, or
  repeated posts.
- Always disclose that Ehsan is Nick's developer.
- Describe Nick as an open-source local security monitor and scanner, not a
  guarantee or a replacement for every security control.
- Use real app footage and measured counts. Never stage a threat as a genuine
  infection or claim an unmeasured false-positive rate.
- A quiet week is acceptable when no community discussion is a genuine fit.
