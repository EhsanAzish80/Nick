prevents the plan from turning into an endless launch campaign.

## Demo storyboard

1. 0–5s: open the signed app to Simple Home; show one protection state.
2. 5–15s: run Quick Check; keep the cursor still so progress is legible.
3. 15–25s: open Activity and one plain-language alert.
4. 25–35s: reveal Technical Details, then switch to Advanced mode.
5. 35–48s: start Deep Scan and show found/checked counts updating.
6. 48–60s: end on “Free, open source, local-first” plus the GitHub URL.

No voiceover claim should exceed what is visible in the recording.
